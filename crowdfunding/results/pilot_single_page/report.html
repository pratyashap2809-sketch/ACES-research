Experiment 1 · Probability of funding under each scenario

The agent opens two fundraisers and can fund only one: the target, with ONE thing changed from the control page, and a rival story left unchanged. P(fund) = share of choices where the target won. The control row (both pages unchanged) is the baseline; the change vs control is the effect of that one feature. Stated probability = the model's own confidence that the target is the better choice. Stars mark changes that stay significant after Holm correction across all scenarios (* p<0.05, ** p<0.01, *** p<0.001).
